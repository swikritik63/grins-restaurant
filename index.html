Our story

Made for San Marcos

Grins opened on Valentine’s Day in 1975 and grew from a small local restaurant into a community gathering place. The restaurant is known for burgers, jumbo onion rings, chicken fried steak, margaritas, private dining rooms, and its tree-shaded deck.

This redesign keeps that friendly local personality while making important information faster to find.
